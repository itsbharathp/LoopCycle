From bottle to bag in four steps

  1. Collected

    Registered pickers bring sorted bottles to one of our 12 hubs. They are weighed openly and paid fair-trade rates the same day.

  2. Washed

    At our Bandung plant the bottles go through a hot wash and the labels come off.

  3. Spun into yarn

    Clean PET is shredded into flake, melted and drawn into fibre.

  4. Woven

    The yarn is woven into fabric, then cut and stitched into a tote in sea-green, indigo or sand.

Made for every day

Strong handles and a fabric that wipes clean. It holds a week of market shopping, a laptop and lunch, or a gym kit.

Care is simple: wash cold with mild soap and dry it in the shade. Skip the bleach and the tumble dryer, and it will last for years.

A supply chain that pays pickers fairly and can prove it

LoopCycle Nusantara is a waste-to-value social enterprise based in Bandung, founded in 2015. We bring informal waste pickers into a recycling supply chain where every kilo is registered, weighed openly and traceable.

We buy sorted plastic from 3,500 registered pickers at fair-trade rates, wash and pelletise it, and sell recycled resin to FMCG packaging makers. Our team of 160 runs 12 collection hubs across West Java. The Loop Tote is the part of that chain you can hold: one bag, 14 bottles.

  1. Pickers3,500 registered, paid fair-trade rates
  2. Hubs12 across West Java, public scales
  3. PlantBandung: wash, flake, pelletise
  4. Packaging makersBuy recycled resin

Fair pay, same day

Pickers are weighed on public scales, paid by grade and paid the same day.

Traceable by design

Each batch links back to its hub and the pickers who collected it, so partners can trust what goes into their packaging.

Respect for the work

We say waste pickers, never scavengers. Pickers are partners with registered IDs, fair rates and a voice.
